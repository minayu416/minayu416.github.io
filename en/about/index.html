生活理念 · Philosophy
Zhuangzi Philosophy × Wabi-Sabi
侘寂の美
I resonate with the Zhuangzi spirit — 'a soul full of wonder', 'a pursuit of freedom', and 'transcending utilitarian thinking'. It means living authentically, free from rigid frameworks, without comparison, without wasting time conforming to others' standards — always returning to the essence and original intention of things.

Wabi-Sabi originates from Japanese Zen Buddhism, finding beauty in simplicity, nature, and stillness — seven aesthetic principles in all. Applied to design and life: strip away unnecessary ornamentation so structure can emerge naturally; embrace imperfection and difference, which allows life to reveal a more honest and genuine form.
簡素・渋い
Simplicity · Astringency
Remove the noise and let the essence surface on its own. There is no need for elaborate decoration or flashy presentation to mask your true character and beauty.
不均斉・自然
Asymmetry · Nature
Go with the flow of nature — patient, steady effort accumulates into deep capability and rich experience over time. Nature is never perfectly symmetrical, and neither are we. There is no need to chase perfection; accepting your own differences makes you more authentic.
幽玄・靜寂
Subtlety · Stillness
In a world of rapid pace and overwhelming information, preserve an inner stillness — and return to yourself.
